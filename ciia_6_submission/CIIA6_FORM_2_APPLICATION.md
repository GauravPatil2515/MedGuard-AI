```text
SAFE CAP⁺ bridges physical child-resistant medication packaging with an advanced neuro-symbolic clinical safety engine to prevent polypharmacy fatalities before pills are ingested. 

Traditional health applications act as basic alarms without assessing whether a drug combination triggers fatal interactions. SAFE CAP⁺ integrates deep learning Graph Neural Networks (Tox21 GSAT for 12 endocrine/stress pathways, ClinTox for clinical trial failure prediction) and Random Forest models (ROC-AUC 0.8627) to screen for cardiac hERG QT-prolongation and lethal CYP3A4/CYP2C9 enzyme-mediated interactions (such as Warfarin-NSAID hemorrhage risks).
```
