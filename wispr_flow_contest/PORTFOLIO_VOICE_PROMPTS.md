## 2. Voice Prompts to Dictate into Wispr Flow on Emergent

When in Emergent, activate Wispr Flow dictation and speak these prompt sections:

### Prompt 1: Hero & Theme
> *"Create a sleek, high-contrast dark-mode developer and growth leader portfolio. At the top, include a header with a glowing badge that says 'Built 100% by Voice using Wispr Flow and Emergent'. The hero headline should be: 'Gaurav Patil — Building Voice-Native AI & Product Growth for India'. Add a subheadline: 'Full-stack builder specializing in neuro-symbolic AI, vernacular voice interfaces, and high-velocity product distribution.' Add two primary call-to-action buttons: 'Explore MedGuard AI Case Study' and 'View India Growth Playbook'."*

### Prompt 2: MedGuard AI Deep-Dive Card
> *"Below the hero, create a featured product showcase for 'MedGuard AI — Beyond the Pill Reminder'. Highlight that it is a regulatory-grade neuro-symbolic platform featuring: 
> 1. Vernacular Audio Alerts in Marathi, Hindi, and English.
> 2. Neuro-symbolic multi-drug cardiotoxicity prediction with a 95% conformal confidence interval.
> 3. Indian Brand Normalization mapping drugs like Ecosprin and Met-XL to active SMILES molecules.
> Add three interactive statistic callout badges: 'ROC-AUC 0.8627 on hERG ML Model', '100% Deterministic Symbolic Rules', and '21 CFR Part 11 Cryptographic Audit Trail'."*

### Prompt 3: Voice-First Philosophy for India
> *"Add a clean section titled: 'Why Voice is the Future of Computing in India'. Explain that in a country with hundreds of dialects and diverse literacy levels, voice dictation isn't just a convenience—it is an accessibility imperative. Highlight how pairing Wispr Flow's noise-resilient dictation with real-time AI tools enables anyone from doctors in Bengaluru clinics to elderly patients in Tier-2 cities to interact effortlessly with technology."*

### Prompt 4: Wispr Flow India Growth Strategy
> *"Add an interactive growth strategy section titled: 'Wispr Flow India Expansion Playbook'. Break it into three pillars:
> 1. Developer & Vibe-Coding Distribution: Tap into the booming AI developer ecosystem in Bengaluru and Hyderabad by integrating Wispr Flow with Cursor and Emergent workflows.
> 2. Campus & Hackathon Partnerships: Run campus-wide voice-coding hackathons across premier engineering universities to capture early-adopter students.
> 3. Niche Professional Verticals: Target medical transcriptionists, legal teams, and vernacular content creators who need fast, zero-latency Hinglish voice-to-text."*

### Prompt 5: Footer & Social Links
> *"Add a clean, minimalist footer with links to my GitHub profile, LinkedIn, and email, alongside an interactive button to schedule a quick conversation."*
