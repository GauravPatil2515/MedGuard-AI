    \textbf{\textcolor{primary}{Quick Overview for Everyone:}} \\
    Many patients forget to take their daily medications (like blood sugar or blood pressure tablets) at the right time. \textbf{SAFE CAP\textsuperscript{+}} links a physical child-resistant medicine bottle to a simple smartphone app using a QR code. It gives patients clear daily reminders, easy-to-read medicine guides, and notifies family members if an essential dose is missed.

\section{The Real-World Problem}
Chronic illnesses like diabetes and hypertension require strict daily medication regimens to remain effective:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Missed or Delayed Doses:} Daily distractions, busy schedules, and aging cause frequent forgotten doses.
    \item \textbf{Confusing Medical Jargon:} Prescription inserts are long, technical, and hard to understand.
    \item \textbf{Child Safety Risks:} Traditional bottles are often accidentally opened by curious young children at home.
    \item \textbf{Lack of Caregiver Awareness:} Family members only learn about missed doses when health complications arise.
\end{itemize}

\section{The SAFE CAP\textsuperscript{+} Solution}
SAFE CAP\textsuperscript{+} combines physical packaging with digital care in four straightforward pillars:

\begin{center}

\hline
\rowcolor{primary!10}
\textbf{Component} & \textbf{Everyday Benefit for Patients and Families} \\
\hline
\textbf{1. Child-Resistant Smart Packaging} & Push-and-turn bottle caps prevent accidental child poisoning while keeping medications safe and dry. \\
\hline
\textbf{2. 1-Scan QR Code Onboarding} & No typing required. A quick scan of the bottle with any smartphone camera opens the app directly to that specific medicine. \\
\hline
\textbf{3. Plain-Language Medicine Guide} & Explains what the pill does, when to take it (e.g., with breakfast), potential side effects, and safe storage in simple words. \\
\hline
\textbf{4. Smart Reminders \& Caregiver Bridge} & Timely notifications with a 1-click ``Mark as Taken'' button. If an important dose is missed, caregivers are immediately notified. \\
\hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\section{Patient Journey (Example: Glimepiride 2 mg)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
    \item \textbf{At the Pharmacy:} The patient receives their prescribed bottle of 30 tablets with the SAFE CAP\textsuperscript{+} QR label and child-proof cap.
    \item \textbf{At Home:} The patient scans the bottle's QR code with their mobile phone.
    \item \textbf{Instant Setup:} The app instantly loads the medicine profile: \textit{``Glimepiride 2 mg — Take 1 tablet daily with breakfast.''}
    \item \textbf{Daily Alert:} At 8:00 AM, the phone sounds a friendly reminder.
    \item \textbf{1-Click Confirmation:} The patient takes the tablet and taps \textbf{``Mark as Taken''}.
    \item \textbf{Safety Backup:} If the dose is forgotten, a real-time alert is escalated to a designated caregiver so they can gently check in.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Implementation Roadmap (Step-by-Step)}

\subsection{Phase 1: Plain-Language Medicine Knowledge Base}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{What it does:} Translates complex chemical details into simple answers:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{Purpose:} e.g., ``Helps your pancreas naturally release insulin after food.''
        \item \textit{How to Take:} e.g., ``Take with breakfast or your first main meal. Do not skip meals.''
        \item \textit{Side Effects:} e.g., ``Signs of low blood sugar: shaking, dizziness, hunger.''
        \item \textit{Storage:} e.g., ``Store below 25$^\circ$C in a dry place. Keep child-resistant cap locked.''
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Vernacular Audio:} Spoken voice guides in \textbf{Marathi}, \textbf{Hindi}, and \textbf{English} for elderly patients who prefer listening over reading.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Phase 2: Smart QR Code Generator \& Mobile Scanner}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Bottle QR Generator:} Creates unique packaging QR codes encoding the drug identity, prescribed dosage, and batch verification.
    \item \textbf{In-App Scanner:} Works seamlessly via any phone camera inside the web app with zero app store installation hurdles.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Phase 3: Clean, Friendly Patient Interface (PWA)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
    \item Large, easy-to-read text designed with high contrast for older adults.
    \item Prominent \textbf{``Take Dose Now''} button with haptic or audio feedback.
    \item Visual \textbf{Pill Inventory Tracker} (e.g., \textit{``6 tablets left in your bottle — Time to refill''}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Phase 4: Family \& Caregiver Guardian Network}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
    \item Real-Time Alerts: Sends immediate notifications to a caregiver's phone when high-priority medications are missed.
    \item Verified History Log: Keeps an accurate record of taken doses that can be shared with treating doctors at clinic visits.
\end{itemize}
